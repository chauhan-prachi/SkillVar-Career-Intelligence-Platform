Not all skill signals are equal

SkillVar keeps self-reported skills, resume mentions and assessment results distinct. Each contributes differently to the match estimate.

  • Self-reportedA skill you say you know.
  • Resume evidenceA skill mentioned in experience or projects.
  • Assessment-testedA skill evaluated through questions or executed SQL.
Important: SkillVar scores are estimates for a specific role, not hiring probabilities or ATS scores. They are designed to help users inspect evidence and identify learning gaps.
