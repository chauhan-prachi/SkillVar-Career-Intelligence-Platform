Understand your evidence

SkillVar considers three types of skill evidence:

  • Self-reported Skills you declare.
  • Resume evidence Skills found in your resume.
  • Assessment results Skills demonstrated through tests.
Important: SkillVar scores are estimates, not the probability of getting hired, a guarantee of employment, or ATS scores. Use them to understand your evidence and identify learning gaps.
